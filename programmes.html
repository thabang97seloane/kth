Computer Basics & MS Office

Our core computer literacy course for anyone starting from zero. You learn to use a computer confidently, then master the Microsoft Office tools employers ask for.

  • Level: Beginner
  • Format: In-person, small class
  • Schedule: Weekday & Saturday classes
  • Outcome: Certificate of completion

What you'll learn

  • Switching on, mouse, keyboard and typing skills
  • Files, folders, USB drives and saving your work
  • MS Word: letters, CVs, tables and formatting
  • MS Excel: budgets, stock lists, formulas and charts
  • MS PowerPoint: build and present a slideshow
  • Internet, Gmail/email, attachments and online safety

CV & Job Application Support

Stand out to employers. We help you create a professional CV and cover letter, and show you how to find and apply for jobs online — including government posts.

  • Level: All levels

  • Cover letters that match the job

NSFAS, University & Bursary Application Help

Online applications can be confusing. We sit with you, step by step, to complete NSFAS, university, TVET college and bursary applications correctly and on time.

  • Level: All levels
  • Format: One-on-one support
  • Bring: ID, results, proof of income
  • Outcome: Application submitted

What you'll learn

  • Creating a myNSFAS account and applying
  • University and TVET college online applications
  • Finding and applying for bursaries
  • Scanning, certifying guidance and uploading documents
  • Creating a professional email address
  • Tracking your application status
